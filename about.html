Designer

That project is probably the clearest example of how I work: I design it, then I build it.

Client Success

From May 2024 to July 2026 I worked as a Client Success Specialist at ActiveDEMAND, a marketing automation platform. I onboarded and trained clients, built landing pages, emails and workflows for them, and wrote support articles and training materials for clients and new team members.

After about a year I moved up to handling Tier 2 tickets, meaning the more complex or unusual issues that needed a closer look. I also worked with the team on making parts of that process faster and clearer.

Before that I spent a few years in retail at BuyBuy Baby, Home Depot and Twisted Goods. That's where I really started noticing how much bad software affects the people who have to use it every day, which is what eventually led me to the Omni redesign project.

AI Tools

I use AI pretty regularly at this point, but for pretty different things depending on the tool. Claude is where I go for anything that needs careful reasoning, writing code, working through a complex design or product problem, or anything where the output actually matters and I need to be able to trust it. This portfolio site was built with it.

Gemini is more of an everyday tool for me. General questions, looking something up quickly, practicing ideas, getting a second read on something. It's more like having a fast and fairly reliable reference on hand.

The thing I've noticed using both is that the tools are good at speeding up execution, but they don't replace the judgment call. You still have to know what you're trying to build and why, otherwise the output is just fast noise.

Claude

Coding, complex problem solving and anything where the output needs to be right

Gemini

Everyday questions, general knowledge and quick practice or reference

AI doesn't replace the thinking. Every decision about what to build and why still has to come from somewhere. The tools just help you get there faster once you know what you're after.
